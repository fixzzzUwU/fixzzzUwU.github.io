FAQ

1. What is the best way to combine different boho patterns without making the room feel cluttered?

Mix a dominant pattern (like a large, subtle print) with solid‑color accessories. Keep the color palette limited to 3–4 tones so the room feels cohesive.

2. How can I maintain a luxe feel while staying on a budget?

Focus on high‑impact pieces such as a statement rug, a unique mirror, or a striking accent chair. Use inexpensive textiles and DIY projects to add texture and personality.

3. Are there any quick updates that can refresh a living room for the 2024 season?

Swap out seasonal throw pillows, add a fresh plant arrangement, and change the curtain tie‑dye colors. These small changes keep the space current without a full redesign.
