Boho Living Room Decor Under $50: 20 Aesthetic Finds

Creating a bohemian living room that feels curated, cozy, and personal doesn't have to drain your bank account. The boho aesthetic is all about layering textures, mixing earthy tones, and showcasing pieces that feel collected over time—not bought in one expensive haul. Whether you're styling a rental with temporary-friendly solutions or just want to refresh your space for the season, there are plenty of stylish options that won't break the bank.

We've rounded up 20 of the best boho living room decor pieces under $50. From statement wall art to plush throw pillows and ambient lighting, these finds will help you achieve that effortlessly eclectic look without the designer price tag.

Start with a Statement: Boho Wall Art

Blank walls are the fastest way to make a living room feel unfinished. Boho wall art is the perfect way to inject color, pattern, and personality into your space. The key is to mix different pieces—think woven textures, earthy prints, and macramé accents—to create a gallery wall that feels organic.

1. Woven Wall Baskets

Nothing says boho quite like natural fiber wall decor. A set of woven baskets in varying sizes adds instant texture and warmth. They're lightweight, easy to hang, and look great clustered together or as a single focal point above a sofa.

2. Abstract Earth Tone Prints

Look for a set of abstract prints featuring warm terracotta, sage green, and sandy beige tones. A multi-piece set allows you to fill a large wall space affordably. Choose frames in natural wood or matte black to keep the look grounded.

3. Macramé Wall Hanging

A classic macramé piece brings a soft, handcrafted feel to any room. Whether you choose a large statement piece for above the couch or a smaller accent for a reading nook, this is a staple of bohemian home decor ideas.

4. Vintage-Style Botanical Prints

For a more eclectic vibe, consider vintage-inspired botanical or celestial prints. These add a touch of whimsy and sophistication, breaking up the heavier textures with a bit of airy illustration.

5. Tapestry Throw

If you're renting and can't paint or put too many holes in the wall, a large tapestry is your best friend. Drape it over a sofa, use it as a bedspread, or hang it with clip rings for an instant, renter-friendly boho wall art solution.

Layer the Comfort: Boho Throw Pillows

The quickest way to transform a basic sofa into a boho haven is with throw pillows. The secret is to mix patterns, textures, and sizes. Don't be afraid to combine a chunky knit with a velvet print or a kilim pattern with a solid faux suede.

6. Chunky Knit Pillow Covers

These add a cozy, tactile element that is essential for a hygge-inspired boho space. They come in neutral creams and warm greys, perfect for layering.

7. Kilim Pattern Pillows

Bold, geometric kilim patterns are a cornerstone of boho decor. They introduce visual interest and a global-inspired feel. Look for covers with hidden zippers for easy washing.

8. Faux Suede Pillows

Suede adds a touch of understated luxury and warmth. A solid terracotta or rust-colored suede pillow will anchor your color palette and feel incredibly soft.

9. Tassel and Fringe Accents

Pillows with tassels or fringe trim are a small detail that makes a big impact. They add movement and that "collected over time" look that defines boho style.

10. Cotton Canvas Pillows with Inspirational Quotes

For a more personal touch, find pillows with simple, earthy quotes or sun and moon motifs. These are great conversation starters and add a layer of personality to your boho living room decor.

Set the Mood: Candles and Ambiance

Lighting is everything in a boho space. You want to create a warm, inviting glow that feels relaxing. Candles are an affordable and effective way to achieve this, adding both scent and style.

11. Scented Candles in Earthy Scents

Look for candles with scents like sandalwood, palo santo, or amber. These fragrances are synonymous with the bohemian lifestyle and instantly make your home feel like a sanctuary. Choose candles in amber glass jars or tin containers for a rustic look.

12. Decorative Candle Holders

Elevate your candle game with unique holders. Think hammered metal, carved wood, or geometric ceramic designs. These pieces act as sculptural decor even when the candle isn't lit.

13. LED Candles with Remote

If you're not a fan of open flames (or have pets that knock things over), LED candles are a safe and realistic alternative. Many now come with a flickering flame effect and a remote control, making it easy to set the mood from your couch.

14. Incense Holder and Sticks

For a more traditional boho vibe, an incense holder with a brass finish is a beautiful and functional piece. It adds a sensory element to your decor that is both calming and authentic.

Finishing Touches: Accents and Textiles

The final layer of your boho living room is all about the details. These are the pieces that tie the room together and make it feel lived-in and loved.

15. Faux Fur Throw Blanket

Drape a faux fur throw over the arm of your sofa or the back of a chair. It adds a luxurious texture that contrasts beautifully with woven baskets and cotton pillows. It's also perfect for cozy movie nights.

16. Ceramic Vases

A set of ceramic vases in organic shapes and earthy glazes is a must-have. Use them alone as sculptural decor or fill them with dried pampas grass or eucalyptus for a low-maintenance, boho touch.

17. Dried Pampas Grass

Speaking of pampas grass, it is the unofficial plant of the boho movement. A bundle of dried pampas grass in a tall vase adds height, softness, and a neutral color palette that works with any scheme.

18. Woven Storage Baskets

Storage doesn't have to be ugly. Large woven baskets are perfect for hiding throw blankets, extra pillows, or kids' toys while adding a natural element to your floor space. They are functional and stylish.

19. Decorative Trays

Use a decorative tray on your coffee table or ottoman to corral remotes, coasters, and candles. A wooden or rattan tray instantly organizes your space and adds a layer of intentional styling.

20. Tabletop Planters

Bring some life into your space with small tabletop planters. Look for pots in terracotta, ceramic, or woven rattan. Even low-maintenance plants like snake plants or succulents add a vital, organic element to your boho living room decor.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the essential elements of boho living room decor?

The essential elements include natural materials like wood, rattan, and jute; layered textiles such as throw pillows and rugs; warm earthy colors like terracotta, olive, and cream; and personal touches like plants, candles, and unique wall art. The goal is a relaxed, collected, and eclectic look.

How can I make my living room look boho on a tight budget?

Focus on high-impact, low-cost changes. Swap out your throw pillow covers, add a textured throw blanket, and introduce a few decorative accents like candles and ceramic vases. Thrift stores are also great places to find unique baskets, frames, and decor pieces that fit the boho aesthetic.

Is boho decor renter-friendly?

Absolutely. Many boho elements are renter-friendly, such as using removable command hooks for lightweight wall art, draping tapestries instead of painting, and using floor pillows and poufs instead of permanent furniture. Woven baskets and plants are also great ways to add style without causing any damage to your walls or floors.
