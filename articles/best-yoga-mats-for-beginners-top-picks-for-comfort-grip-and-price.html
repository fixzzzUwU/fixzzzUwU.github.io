Best Yoga Mats for Beginners: Top Picks for Comfort, Grip, and Price

So you've decided to start yoga. Congratulations. Then you hit the first hurdle: choosing a mat. It seems simple, but between the super-thin travel mats and the extra-plush ones that feel like a mattress, it's easy to feel overwhelmed. This guide keeps things simple. I'll walk you through what actually matters—comfort, grip, and price—and then share my picks for the best yoga mats for beginners on the market today.

How to Choose Your First Yoga Mat

Before we get to the list, let's cover the basics. Understanding these three factors will make you a much smarter shopper.

Thickness: When a Thick Yoga Mat for Knee Pain Is the Right Call

Thickness is measured in millimeters. The thinnest mats are around 1.5mm and are made for travel. The thickest go up to 12mm or more.

  • 3mm (1/8 inch): Good for balance, not much cushioning.
  • 5–6mm (1/4 inch): The sweet spot for most beginners.
  • 8–12mm (3/8–1/2 inch): Plush support for sensitive joints.

If you have tender knees, a thick yoga mat for knee pain is non-negotiable. A 6mm or thicker mat will protect your joints in kneeling poses like camel or low lunge. The only downside? Thicker mats can feel slightly less stable in one-legged balancing poses. Start with 6mm and adjust from there as you learn your preferences.

Grip: What Makes a Best Non Slip Yoga Mat

Nothing ruins a good flow like your hands sliding forward in downward dog. When people search for the "best non slip yoga mat," they usually fall into two groups:

  • Dry grippers: You want a closed-cell surface like PVC or TPE. It feels sticky and smooth even when your palms are clammy.
  • Sweaty palms: Open-cell natural rubber mats offer incredible grip when wet, but they absorb sweat and require more maintenance.

Don't forget to check the bottom of the mat too. If it slides on your floor, the base texture is wrong. A quality mat should stay put on hardwood, tile, and even carpet.
