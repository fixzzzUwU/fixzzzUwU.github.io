How to Maximize Credit Card Rewards

Earning cash back is one thing, but maximizing it takes a bit of strategy. Here are a few proven tips to get the most from your cash back credit cards in 2025:

  • Pay your balance in full each month. Interest charges will quickly wipe out any rewards you earn. Cash back is only a benefit if you're not carrying a balance.
  • Stack your cards. Use a flat-rate card for everything, then add a bonus category card for groceries, gas, or dining. This way, you're always earning at least the base rate, with elevated rates where it matters.
  • Put bills on autopay. Many cards treat utility, internet, and subscription payments as regular purchases. Set up autopay to earn cash back on bills you're already paying.
  • Take advantage of sign-up bonuses. A welcome bonus can be worth hundreds of dollars in cash back. Just make sure you can hit the spending requirement without overspending.
  • Set calendar reminders. Some cards offer rotating bonus categories that change quarterly. Mark your calendar and activate the bonus each quarter to avoid missing out.
