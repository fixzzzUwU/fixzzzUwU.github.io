Affordable Home Decor Ideas That Look Expensive

There's a misconception that making your home look expensive requires a big budget. In reality, the most polished spaces are usually the result of thoughtful styling, not deep pockets. Whether you're renting your first apartment or just want to refresh your living room without the guilt, small decor accents can completely transform how a space feels. The trick is choosing items that add texture, warmth, and a sense of intention — things like throw pillows, wall art, candles, and vases. If you're looking for cheap home decor ideas that don't look cheap, this list is for you.

We've rounded up some of the best affordable home decor ideas that help you build a home decor aesthetic that feels high-end, even on a rental budget. And the best part? Most of these picks fall into the living room decor under $50 category.

Start With Throw Pillows: The Quickest Room Reset

Throw pillows are the easiest, most affordable home decor upgrade you can make. They instantly add color, texture, and a sense of comfort to any sofa, chair, or bed. But there's a method to making them look expensive rather than cluttered.

Use Odd Numbers and Vary Textures

Designers rarely use matching pillow sets. Instead, they layer different sizes, shapes, and fabrics. A good rule of thumb is to style with an odd number — three or five pillows usually looks right. Mix a smooth linen cover with a chunky knit or a velvet option. The contrast in textures is what gives your sofa a curated, expensive feel.

If you're shopping on a budget, look for pillow covers rather than buying new pillows every time. Covers are cheaper, easy to store, and you can swap them with the seasons to change your apartment decor on a budget. Pair them with high-quality pillow inserts so they stay plump and substantial.

Choose Wall Art That Feels Curated

Wall art is another powerful way to elevate a room. But the wrong wall art — think tiny, scattered frames or overly generic prints — can actually make a space feel cheaper. The secret is to go bold and intentional.

Go Big or Go Gallery Style

In a living room, one oversized piece of canvas wall art can anchor an entire wall. It creates the kind of focal point that looks like it came from a high-end furniture store. If you prefer a gallery wall, keep the frames consistent — same color, same style — and use black-and-white prints for a timeless look.

This is one of the best cheap home decor ideas because framed prints and canvas art are surprisingly affordable, especially when you shop online. Look for pieces with simple, abstract designs in neutral tones. They blend with any color palette and always look more expensive than they are.

Use Candles to Create Instant Ambiance

Lighting is the most underrated tool in interior design. Harsh overhead lights can make a room feel sterile, while warm, dim lighting makes everything look softer and more inviting. Candles are the simplest way to achieve that cozy, expensive glow.

Group Candles at Different Heights

Instead of one lone candle on a coffee table, arrange a cluster of pillar candles at varying heights. The visual rhythm instantly creates a home decor aesthetic that feels deliberate and polished. You can also place a large candle inside a glass lantern or on a decorative tray to add even more style points.

If you're worried about candles in a rental, flameless LED candles in warm tones are a safe and affordable alternative. They give off the same glow without the scent or the fire hazard.

Style Vases Like Decorative Sculptures

Vases are one of those decor items that people either overdo or completely ignore. When styled correctly, they become sculptural objects that add height and personality to a room.

Keep It Simple With Stems or Branches

Skip the giant, overstuffed floral arrangements. A single ceramic vase with a few stems of dried pampas grass, eucalyptus, or even simple twigs looks far more intentional and expensive. The empty space around the stems is part of the design.

Place a vase on a console table, a bookshelf, or the corner of a dining table. Grouping two or three vases of different heights together can also create a lovely vignette. This is one of the easiest affordable home decor ideas for renters, because vases don't require any permanent changes to your walls or floors.

FAQ

How can I make my apartment look expensive on a budget?

Focus on a few high-impact accents rather than filling the room with lots of small items. Invest in textured throw pillows, one oversized piece of wall art, and a few candles or vases to create warmth and depth. Styling everything in odd numbers and consistent tones makes the space feel curated.

What are the best cheap home decor ideas for renters?

Renter-friendly options include interchangeable throw pillow covers, frameless canvas art (no nails needed), pillar candles, and vases with dried or faux stems. These items are lightweight, easy to move, and don't require drilling holes or repainting walls — which makes them perfect for apartment decor on a budget.

Where should I spend more vs. save when decorating a living room?

Save on trend-forward pieces like pillow covers, candle holders, and artificial stems, since you'll likely swap them out frequently. Spend a little more on items you use daily and want to last, such as quality pillow inserts, a sturdy ceramic vase, or a large canvas print that anchors the whole room.
