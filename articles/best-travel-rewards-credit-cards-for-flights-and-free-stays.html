Why Travel Rewards Cards Beat Ordinary Cash Back

Travel cards offer something regular cash-back cards don't: transferable points. That means you can move your points to airline and hotel partners, often unlocking far more value per dollar than a flat 1.5% cash return. For example, a $500 flight booked through a card portal might only cost 35,000 points, while transferring those same points to an airline could get you a ticket worth $1,200.

Of course, not all travel cards are created equal. Some earn big on airfare but barely on dining. Others offer luxury perks but hit you with high annual fees. The key is matching a card to your spending habits and travel style.

How to Choose the Best Credit Card for Flights and Hotels

Before we dive into the best picks, here are the three factors that matter most:

  1. Earning rate: Look for cards that offer at least 2x points on travel purchases and 3x or more on dining and groceries.
  2. Redemption flexibility: Cards that let you transfer points to multiple airlines and hotels give you far more value than those locked into a single portal.
  3. Fees and credits: A card with a $95 annual fee can still be worth it if it includes a $100 travel credit. Meanwhile, the best no annual fee travel card should have no blackout dates and no foreign transaction fees.

Pro tip: if you want to learn how to earn free flights, focus on sign-up bonuses. A single welcome offer often equals one free domestic flight or even a short international trip.

How to Earn Free Flights Fast

Now that you’ve seen the best travel credit cards 2025 lineup, here’s how to maximize them:

  • Stack sign-up bonuses. Apply for one card, hit the minimum spend, collect the bonus, then move on to the next card after a few months. This is the fastest way to rack up enough points for a trip.
  • Use shopping portals. Most card issuers have online shopping portals that offer 5 to 15 extra points per dollar at major retailers. Always check the portal before you buy anything online.
  • Refer friends. Many cards give you 10,000 to 20,000 bonus points for each friend who approves. It’s a surprisingly easy way to earn free flights without spending an extra cent.
  • Don’t obsess over transfer ratios. Sometimes booking directly through the card’s travel portal is a better deal than transferring points to an airline. Do the math on a few example redemptions to know which one works best.
