What Makes a Legging Truly Squat-Proof?

Before we jump into the list, let's talk about what actually separates a great pair from a frustrating one. A truly squat-proof legging is more than just dark fabric. It comes down to three things:

  • Fabric weight and opacity: If you can stretch the fabric between your hands and see your skin, it will be sheer in a deep squat. The best pairs use thick, densely knit materials that stay opaque even when pulled taut.
  • Four-way stretch with recovery: You want fabric that moves with you but snaps back into place. Nylon and elastane blends (usually around 75–80% nylon, 20–25% spandex) offer the best balance of stretch and structure.
  • A waistband that stays where it belongs: High-waisted doesn't automatically mean no-roll. The best waistbands use a wider band and strategic seaming or a drawstring to grip without digging in.

Keep these factors in mind as you browse the options below.

How to Make Your Leggings Last Longer

Once you find your perfect pair, you'll want to take care of them. Proper maintenance is key to keeping them squat-proof and buttery soft for years.

  • Wash cold, always. Hot water breaks down the elastane fibers, which leads to stretching out and pilling.
  • Hang to dry. The heat from a dryer is the number one killer of leggings. Air-drying them flat will preserve their shape, elasticity, and opacity.
  • Flip them inside out. Washing leggings inside out protects the outer surface from friction against other clothes, which is what causes pilling.

FAQ: Your Squat-Proof Legging Questions Answered

1. How can I tell if my leggings are actually squat-proof?

The most reliable way is the stretch test. Take the fabric at the thigh or hip and stretch it as far as it will go with your hands. If you can see your skin through the stretched fabric, they will be see-through when you squat. Look for leggings with a high nylon and elastane blend (about 75–80% nylon) for the best opacity.

2. What is the difference between buttery soft leggings and compression leggings?

Buttery soft leggings, like the Lululemon Align or CRZ Yoga Butterluxe, are made with a light, brushed fabric designed for low-impact activities, comfort, and lounging. They offer minimal to no compression. Compression leggings, like Gymshark Vital or Lululemon Wunder Train, use a tighter, more structured knit to hug your muscles. They provide support, improve blood flow during workouts, and have a sculpting effect on the body.

3. What are the best high waisted gym leggings for tall women?

Look for leggings that specify a "tall" or "long" inseam option. Brands like Lululemon (through their online store), Gymshark, and CRZ Yoga often offer longer inseams. If you're over 5'8", we recommend checking the inseam measurement in the product description—aim for at least 28 inches to ensure the leggings reach your ankle.
