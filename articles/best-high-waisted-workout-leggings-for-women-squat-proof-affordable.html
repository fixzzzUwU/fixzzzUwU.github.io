What to Look for in High-Quality Leggings

Before diving into our top picks, it helps to understand what separates a mediocre pair of leggings from a great one. When shopping for squat proof leggings, keep these three factors in mind:

1. Fabric and Opacity

The "squat test" is the gold standard for a reason. You need fabric that is thick enough to be opaque when stretched but breathable enough to prevent overheating. Look for nylon and Spandex (Elastane) blends. Four-way stretch fabric is essential for flexibility, ensuring the material moves with you, not against you.

2. The Waistband Construction

A high waistband isn't just about style; it's about support. The best high waisted leggings feature a wide, contoured waistband that sits at your natural waist. This design prevents the dreaded "roll-down" during high-intensity interval training (HIIT) or yoga. Look for a waistband with a hidden pocket for keys or cards—a small detail that makes a big difference.

3. Stitching and Seams

Cheap leggings often feature a single seam down the front, which can be uncomfortable and unflattering. Better quality options use a gusseted crotch (a diamond-shaped piece of fabric) to improve mobility and reduce chafing. Flat-lock seams are also a sign of quality, as they lie flat against the skin to prevent irritation.

Caring for Your Leggings

To keep your squat proof leggings looking new, you need to care for them properly. Frequent washing can break down the elastane fibers, causing the leggings to become sheer or lose their shape.

  1. Wash Cold: Always use cold water to prevent shrinking and color fading.
  2. Inside Out: Turn your leggings inside out before washing to protect the outer surface from pilling.
  3. No Fabric Softener: Fabric softener clogs the pores of technical fabrics, reducing their moisture-wicking abilities. Use a gentle detergent instead.
  4. Air Dry: Avoid the dryer. Heat is the enemy of stretchy fabrics. Hang your leggings to dry to maintain their compression and fit.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are expensive leggings really better than affordable ones?

Not necessarily. While luxury brands often use proprietary fabrics, many affordable brands now offer similar blends of nylon and Spandex. The key is checking the opacity and stitching quality. As long as the leggings are thick enough to be squat proof leggings and have a sturdy waistband, a lower price point doesn't mean lower quality.

How do I know if my leggings are squat-proof?

The best way to test this is the "bend and stretch" test. Put the leggings on and stand in front of a mirror. Bend your knees deeply into a squat position. If you can see the color of your skin or your underwear through the fabric, they are not squat-proof. If the fabric remains opaque, you are good to go.

What is the difference between high-waisted and mid-rise leggings?

High-waisted leggings sit at or above your belly button, offering more core coverage and support. Mid-rise leggings typically sit just below the belly button. High-waisted styles are generally preferred for workouts because they stay in place better during bending and jumping, and they offer a flattering, smoothing effect on the midsection.
