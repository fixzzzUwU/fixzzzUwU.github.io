17 Cheap Boho Decor Items That Will Transform Your Living Room

There’s something about bohemian style that makes a room feel instantly relaxed, layered, and personal. The best part? You don’t need a big budget to get the look. Start with Macrame Wall Hangings

Macrame is the backbone of boho wall decor. It adds texture, softness, and a handmade feel that instantly warms up blank walls. A macrame wall hanging living room setup is also one of the easiest ways to make a rental feel like yours — no paint, no nails required if you use command hooks.

1. Classic Large Macrame Wall Hanging

This is the showstopper. A large macrame piece hung above your sofa or console table becomes the focal point of the whole room. Look for one with varied knot textures and natural cotton rope. It reads as high-end art, but most options hover well under $40.

2. Set of Two Small Macrame Hangings

If you have a narrow wall or a corner that feels empty, a set of small macrame hangings works beautifully side by side. They add vertical interest without overwhelming the space, and they’re perfect for renters who want flexible boho wall decor ideas.

3. Macrame Shelf with Wooden Dowel

Part wall decor, part storage. A macrame shelf gives you a spot to display crystals, small plants, or candles while contributing to the overall textured look. It’s one of those affordable boho home decor pieces that pulls double duty.

4. Beaded Macrame Wall Art

Add a little sparkle with a macrame piece that includes wooden beads or subtle fringe details. The mixed materials make it feel custom and collected — exactly the vibe bohemian interiors are known for.

Layer in Throw Pillows and Textiles

Boho living rooms thrive on layers. Throw pillows are the easiest way to bring in pattern, color, and comfort, and they’re cheap enough to swap out when you want a refresh.

5. Fringe Throw Pillow Covers

A simple pillow cover with fringe trim instantly reads boho. Buy two and mix them with neutral sofa cushions for a relaxed, gathered look. Just make sure to grab a pillow insert or repurpose ones you already own.

6. Woven Mandala Pillow Cover

Mandala patterns are a classic boho staple. A woven cover in earthy tones like rust, mustard, or olive adds depth to your seating area. These covers typically run between $10 and $20, making them a no-brainer for boho decor on a budget.

7. Chunky Knit Throw Blanket

Okay, it’s not a pillow, but a chunky knit throw draped over the back of your couch is essential for the boho room aesthetic on a budget. It adds warmth and texture, and it gives your pillows something to play off of.

8. Tassel Lumbar Pillow

A lumbar pillow with tassels is perfect for layering in front of larger square pillows. It supports your lower back during movie nights, and the tassel detail adds that handcrafted feel you want in a boho space.

Add Flexible Seating with Poufs and Floor Cushions

Poufs are practically the unofficial chair of boho living rooms. They’re lightweight, versatile, and perfect for small spaces where extra seating is always welcome.

9. Round Woven Natural Fiber Pouf

A round woven pouf made from seagrass or jute is a classic pick. Use it as a footrest, extra seat, or even a side table when you set a tray on top. It’s light enough to move around easily, so it works great in apartments.

10. Boho Floor Cushion Set

Floor cushions are perfect for casual hangouts or creating a cozy reading nook. Grab a set of two or four in coordinating patterns and pile them in a corner with a low table. This is one of the more budget-friendly options for seating that doesn’t take up visual space.

11. Tassel or Embroidered Pouf Ottoman

If you want something with more pattern, look for a pouf with embroidery or a tassel accent. These pieces lean more decorative while still being functional. They’re also a great way to introduce a pop of color into a neutral room.

13. Woven Rattan Wall Basket

FAQ

How do I get the boho look in a living room on a budget?

Start with a few high-impact, low-cost pieces: a macrame wall hanging, throw pillow covers, and a pouf or floor cushion. Mix textures like cotton, jute, and wool, and shop for neutral base pieces you can layer with patterns over time. The boho look is meant to be built gradually, so don’t feel pressured to buy everything at once.

What are the best boho wall decor ideas for small living rooms?

Vertical pieces like tall macrame hangings, wall baskets, and macrame plant hangers work great in small spaces because they draw the eye upward without taking up floor space. A set of two small wall hangings or a cluster of woven baskets can fill a blank wall beautifully without overwhelming a compact room.

Are boho poufs comfortable to sit on every day?

Yes and no. Woven poufs are sturdy and work well for occasional seating or as a footrest, but they don’t offer much back support. For everyday lounging, pair a pouf with a floor cushion or a low-back chair. That way you get the boho look and the comfort you need.
