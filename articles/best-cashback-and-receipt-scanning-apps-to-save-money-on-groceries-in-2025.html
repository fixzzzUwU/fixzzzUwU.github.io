What Are Cashback and Receipt-Scanning Apps?

Cashback apps reward you for purchases you were already going to make. There are two main types:

  • Receipt-scanning apps ask you to snap a photo of your grocery receipt after checkout. The app matches items to available offers and credits your account.
  • Cashback and rewards portals link to your store loyalty accounts or card, so offers activate automatically—no receipt required.

Most people get the best results by using a mix of both. The key is understanding which apps pay out fastest, which have the widest retailer coverage, and which ones actually offer meaningful rewards rather than pennies.

Ibotta remains one of the most well-known names in the grocery cashback space. You browse offers before you shop, add them to your list, then upload your receipt or link your retailer loyalty card to claim the cash back.

The app covers major supermarkets, big-box stores, and even some online grocery services. Payouts are available via PayPal, bank transfer, or gift cards once you hit the minimum threshold.

Cons:

Ibotta vs Fetch: Which Should You Choose?

This is the comparison most shoppers ask about, and the honest answer is that they serve different purposes.

Choose Ibotta if:

  • You're willing to plan your shopping around specific offers
  • You want cash payouts, not just gift cards
  • You shop at major supermarkets regularly

Choose Fetch if:

  • You want zero effort—just scan every receipt
  • You shop at a wide variety of stores
  • You're happy redeeming points for gift cards

Many savvy shoppers use both. Fetch captures the receipts Ibotta doesn't recognize, while Ibotta delivers higher-value cashback on planned purchases. Stacking the two is one of the simplest ways to maximize grocery savings.

Tips to Get the Most Out of Grocery Cashback Apps

  1. Check offers before you shop. Five minutes of planning can mean several dollars back.
  2. Scan every receipt, even small ones. Small purchases add up faster than you'd think.
  3. Stack apps when possible. Use a cashback portal for online orders and a receipt app for in-store trips.
  4. Watch for bonus promotions. Limited-time offers often double or triple your usual rewards.
  5. Cash out regularly. Points and balances can expire or change value—don't let them sit forever.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are receipt-scanning apps safe to use?

Yes, reputable apps like Ibotta, Fetch, and Checkout 51 use secure connections and don't store your payment information. Always download apps from official app stores and read the privacy policy if you're concerned about data use.

How much can I realistically earn with grocery cashback apps?

Earnings vary widely based on how much you shop and how actively you use offers. Casual users might earn a few dollars a month, while dedicated users who plan purchases around offers can earn significantly more. It's best to think of it as a supplement to your budget, not a replacement for it.

Do cashback apps work for online grocery orders?

Many do. Rakuten, Ibotta, and Fetch all support online grocery purchases to varying degrees. Just make sure you link your account or upload your order confirmation as required by each app.
