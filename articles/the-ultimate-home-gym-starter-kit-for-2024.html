Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How much space do I need for a home gym?

A1: A single doorway (about 3 ft × 7 ft) is enough for a pull‑up bar, adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, and a yoga mat. Add a collapsible bench for extra stability.

Q2: Are adjustable dumbbells better than fixed weights?

A2: For most home users, yes. They save space, reduce clutter, and allow gradual progression without buying multiple sets.

Q3: What’s the best way to store resistance bands?

A3: Keep them in a storage bag or zip‑lock pouch. Avoid hanging them for long periods to prevent tangles and maintain elasticity.
