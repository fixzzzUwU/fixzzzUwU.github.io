FAQ

Q1: How can I tell if a piece of furniture is truly eco‑friendly?

A1: Look for certifications such as FSC for wood, GOTS for textiles, and BSCI or Fair Trade for labor practices. Brands that disclose their supply chain and use renewable energy are also strong indicators of genuine sustainability.

Q2: Are reclaimed wood pieces more expensive than new wood?

A2: Reclaimed wood can be pricier due to the labor involved in sourcing, cleaning, and finishing the timber. However, the unique character and reduced environmental impact often justify the extra cost.

Q3: What’s the best way to care for bamboo rugs to extend their life?

A3: Spot‑clean with a mild, pH‑neutral detergent, avoid direct sunlight for prolonged periods, and rotate the rug regularly to prevent uneven wear. Using a rug pad also helps reduce friction and maintain its shape.
