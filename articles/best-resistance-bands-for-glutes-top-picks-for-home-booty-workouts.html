Why Resistance Bands Are a Must-Have for Glute Training

Your glutes are the largest muscle group in your body, and they respond well to consistent tension. Resistance bands provide that tension throughout the entire movement, which is something machines and free weights don't always do. When you use a band for a glute bridge or a squat, the resistance increases as you squeeze at the top, forcing your muscles to work harder where they're strongest.

Bands are also incredibly joint-friendly. Unlike heavy barbells, they don't put compressive stress on your spine or knees, which makes them a great option if you're dealing with back pain or just want to train safely at home.
